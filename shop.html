Atelier Styling Guide

How to Style Fine Necklaces

Curated wisdom from our private salon stylists to assist you in harmonizing metals, proportions, and necklines.

Rule 01

The 3-Tier Layering Harmony

Begin with an anchor choker (14"-16") to frame the collarbone hollow. Add a textured 18" princess chain for dimension, and finish with a 20"-24" pendant or lariat to lengthen the silhouette.

Rule 02

Embrace Mixed Metal Duality

Rules of strict monochrome are obsolete. Modern luxury celebrates intertwining warm 18K Champagne Gold with crisp 925 sterling silver. Anchor the look with our dual-tone Clarisse necklace.

Rule 03

Necklines & Occasion Guidance

Pair plunging V-necklines with dramatic lariats; opt for sculptural silver chokers with strapless evening gowns; and choose delicate diamond solitaires for buttoned silk collars.
