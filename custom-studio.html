Frequently Clarified

Custom Atelier Inquiries

What is the standard production timeline for a bespoke commission?

Because each custom piece is hand-assembled, laser-inscribed, and officially hallmarked in Geneva, standard atelier crafting takes between 12 to 18 business days. We provide photo updates of your piece on the goldsmith workbench throughout the journey.

Can I adjust chain length or re-engrave my piece later?

Yes. All Aurelia custom commissions include complimentary sizing adjustments within the first 12 months. Our Valenza atelier also offers heirloom re-polishing and secondary hallmark additions whenever you choose to mark subsequent milestones.

Are the diamonds and precious metals certified ethical?

Without compromise. 100% of our gold and silver is recycled or sourced from RJC (Responsible Jewellery Council) certified refineries in Switzerland. All diamonds are natural, Kimberley Process compliant, graded VVS1 minimum clarity, and accompanied by full gemological documentation.

How does payment and insured delivery work?

You can commission your custom necklace directly via our secure checkout simulator or salon invoice. Every completed piece is dispatched via armored white-glove courier with discrete tamper-evident packaging and full transit insurance.
