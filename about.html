The Art of the Goldsmith

Where Precision Meets
Pure Passion.

Unlike mass-manufactured chains stamped from hollow foil, every Aurelia piece begins as solid precious ingot. Our proprietary 18K Champagne Gold alloy infuses classical yellow gold with a subtle whisper of palladium—yielding a luminous warmth that compliments every skin undertone.

In sterling silver, we apply an electro-plated rhodium barrier seven times denser than commercial fashion standards, preserving an enduring mirror sheen impervious to atmospheric oxidation.
