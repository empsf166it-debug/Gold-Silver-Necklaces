The Aurelia Philosophy

Crafted by Hand,
Revered Across Generations.

Founded in Geneva in 1994, Maison Aurelia was born out of reverence for the timeless neckpiece. We believe a fine necklace is not merely an ornament—it is an intimate companion that marks milestones, reflects personal sovereignty, and gathers the warmth of the wearer’s skin.

We exclusively utilize certified 100% recycled 18K solid gold, conflict-free VVS diamonds, and sustainably cultured keshi pearls. Every chain link is individually soldered and hand-assayed to guarantee zero pinches and lifelong durability.
