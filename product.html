Preserving Your Heirloom

18K Solid Champagne Gold: Solid gold does not tarnish, yet daily oils and cosmetics can cloud diamond fire. Clean gently using warm water, mild soap, and the complimentary Aurelia micro-fiber polishing brush.

925 Sterling Silver: Our silver creations are sealed with an anti-tarnish rhodium barrier. Store your necklace in the provided airtight anti-oxidation suede pouch when not worn. Avoid wearing during swimming or thermal baths.
